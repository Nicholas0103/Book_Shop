About Our Bookstore

A peek into our history and passion for crime literature

Our Story

Inspired by classic detective novels and Sherlock Holmes' iconic study, our bookstore was built for every fan of mystery and psychological suspense. We noticed local readers lacked a dedicated space for detective fiction, so we built a one-stop sanctuary fully focused on whodunits and locked-room puzzles.
